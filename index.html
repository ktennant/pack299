Every month centers on one of the six aims of Scouting — walked in order, September through April.

Scouts earn advancements, not grades. Each aim is completed by finishing hands-on Adventures — outdoor skills, STEM, crafts, and service — at meetings, on outings, and at home.

The Basics

How Pack 299 Works

When

One pack meeting — first Thursdays of each month, 7:00 PM

Where

Winstead Elementary cafeteria

Outings

Campouts, hikes, service projects, the Pinewood Derby, and much more

Family

We're a family pack, welcoming both boys and girls. Parents, siblings, and grandparents are welcome on nearly every outing.

Cost

Scouting America Membership: $85 annually. Pack Dues: Free for new Scouts.
