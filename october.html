Announcements

Pack Business

  • SOAR Adventure Tower is being rescheduled. We don't have a new date yet — we'll share it on WhatsApp and in a future newsletter as soon as it's set.
  • Pack dues are $35 per Scout for all returning members. First-year Scouts don't pay dues. Venmo @Ross-Lyons-1 with the Scout's name in the message.
  • If you haven't yet, join our WhatsApp Community — it's where pack chatter, reminders, and on-the-trail updates happen.

Advancements

  • Finish Bobcat at home. If your Scout hasn't completed every Bobcat requirement, wrap it up at home and enter it in Scoutbook Plus. Requirement links are in the September newsletter.
  • Use Scoutbook Plus to record your Scout's adventures. It works well on your phone's browser, and the Scouting mobile app is available too.

Volunteers Needed

  • We need parent volunteers to lead our Franklin Christmas Parade entry — theme is "Santa's Helpers — Making Spirits Bright." Email cubmaster@pack299.com to help or share ideas.
  • We also need 2 trucks and 2 trailers for the float. If you can drive or lend one for the day, email cubmaster@pack299.com.
