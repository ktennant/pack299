Dashed dates
Set by an outside organization and not yet confirmed. Don't book travel around them.
Meetings
First Thursday of the month, 7:00 PM, Winstead Elementary cafeteria, unless otherwise communicated. Every event requires a parent or guardian to attend — the whole family is welcome.
Arrow of Light
AOL Scouts cross over to a troop in February, so they run ahead of the pack themes. The monthly aims map onto the six required AOL adventures; Personal Fitness should be finished at home over the winter since it isn't a pack theme until April. Range Day, the fall campout, and the Jamboree each cover elective credit. Target: all six core adventures plus two electives complete before February 4.
Changes
All dates and activities are subject to change with notice.
